# ExampleDesktopObservations

Vital signs over the last 24 hours on the regional early warning chart, focused graphs with thumbnails and critical limits, the readings as a table, and a timeline of the admission and medicines alongside.

## Components used

- PatientBanner
- VitalSignsChart
- ObservationGraph
- GraphThumbnail
- ObservationTable
- TimelineView

## Source

`examples/desktop-observations.tsx` in the package. Open `docs/site/desktop-observations.html` locally to switch region (GB, AU, NZ) and theme.
